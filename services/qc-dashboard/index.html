The problem

Spreadsheets don't catch a batch drifting out of spec.

A small brewery runs QC on a tangle of spreadsheets and gut feel. Readings get transcribed wrong, nobody sees a batch trending off until it's in cans, and yeast health is a guess instead of a number. One dumped batch costs more than this entire build — and the ones you sell off-spec quietly cost your reputation.

The fix isn't more software you won't use. It's a small, focused system that runs the capture → check → alert → learn loop automatically, wired to how you actually brew. That's what this is.

What you get

The whole loop, running whether or not anyone remembers to run it.

01 · Capture

Readings in, once

Gravity, temp, pH, DO, and pitch data captured in one place — entered once, never re-keyed from a clipboard.

02 · Check

Per-style spec bands

Every batch checked against bands built from your history — attenuation-vs-target as the highest-signal alarm.

03 · Alert

The right person, in time

Out-of-spec batches ping you by email, Slack, or SMS — matched to severity, tuned to avoid alarm fatigue.

04 · Learn

An agent, when ready

Optional AI agent compares each batch to your baseline: "Batch 12 is attenuating 8% slow — check pitch rate."

Questions

Before you book.

What if my data is a mess right now?

That's normal, and it's step one. If you're capturing readings anywhere — even a rough spreadsheet — we can build on it. If you're not capturing yet, start with the free calculators and the QC automation guide, then come back.

Do I have to buy the AI agent?

No. The core build is the capture → check → alert loop, which is where most of the value is. The agent (comparing each batch to your baseline) is an optional upgrade you add when you're ready — not a requirement.

Who owns the data and the dashboard?

You do. Full export, no lock-in, and your fermentation data is never resold or shared. In a small industry, that privacy is part of the point.

How is the price fixed if every brewery is different?

The free scoping call is where we nail the scope — tanks, styles, integrations — and I give you a fixed quote in the $1.5k–3k range before any work starts. No hourly billing, no scope creep.
